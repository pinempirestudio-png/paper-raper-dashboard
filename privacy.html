Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

Paper Raper dashboard is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the Google Drive files that this app creates itself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in the Drive.
  • What it does with them: it writes and updates the owner's own dashboard files. Nothing is read from Google beyond those files.
  • Sharing: no data from Google is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is kept only on the owner's own computers. The owner can remove access at any time in their Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access.

Questions: contact the owner on GitHub.
